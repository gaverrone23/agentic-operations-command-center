# Three-minute interview demo

**0:00–0:25 | Business problem**

An operations team must respond to disruptions with incomplete, changing information. Analysts spend time reconciling multiple signals; not every issue should escalate to a manager. This is a fictional scenario designed to test an operating model.

**0:25–1:30 | Demonstrate the workflow**

Select **Accessible Vehicle Disruption** and start. Show the seven modeled roles, shared case state and the investigation progress. Explain that fleet feasibility, rider impacts and policy constraints are examined independently and then reconciled. The simulation is intentionally deterministic.

**1:30–2:10 | Human decision**

At the decision stage, review the prepared partner update. Approve, modify or hold the simulated message. No partner message is ever actually sent. Show the audit entry and the distinction between a completed task and verified operational recovery.

**2:10–2:40 | Grounded assistant**

Open **Ask the case** and ask: “What evidence supports this plan?” The assistant uses only synthetic case context. If a live model key is configured, identify that response as model-generated. Otherwise, identify Reference mode honestly.

**2:40–3:00 | Transferability and limitations**

Switch to the zone-booking or duplicate-trip scenario to illustrate that different incidents activate different roles. Explain that real use would require trustworthy integrations, permission checks, a baseline, and operational validation. No actual ROI or autonomous multi-agent execution is claimed.
